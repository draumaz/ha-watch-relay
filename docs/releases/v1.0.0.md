# WristBridge 1.0.0

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/draumaz/wristbridge/main/docs/social-preview.png" alt="WristBridge: Home Assistant Wear OS tiles, proxied through your phone">
</p>

**WristBridge is a Home Assistant companion for Wear OS that never talks to Home Assistant directly. Every request goes through your phone.**

If your Home Assistant server is only reachable over your phone's VPN (Tailscale, WireGuard, etc.), watch clients that open their own connection stop working as soon as you leave home. WristBridge sidesteps that. The watch app doesn't even request the `INTERNET` permission. It sends each command to the phone over the Wearable Data Layer, and the phone makes the call to Home Assistant using its own network and VPN.

This is the first stable release.

## What's in the box

### 🎛️ Controls tile
- Up to **6 Home Assistant entities** as glyph-only, Material 3 Expressive scalloped buttons, with no header or ON/OFF text.
- A filled scallop means **on**, an outlined one means **off**. Taps respond instantly with a springy bounce, a gear-spin animation, and haptic feedback.
- Sends explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off` calls (no blind `toggle`), so the watch and Home Assistant can't drift out of sync.
- Supports `switch`, `light`, `input_boolean`, `fan`, `lock`, `cover`, `button`, `input_button`, `script` and `scene`.

### 📷 Camera tile + live viewer
- Shows the latest snapshot from any `camera.*` entity. The phone downscales and re-encodes it so it transfers quickly over Bluetooth.
- Tap **Refresh** for a new frame, or **Live** to open a fullscreen viewer that refreshes every **2–30 s** while the screen is on and stops as soon as you leave it.
- Choose between three quality presets: Fast, Balanced and Sharp.

### 📱 Phone companion app
- Paste your Home Assistant URL and Long-Lived Access Token, or **scan the token's QR code**. The scanner runs through Google Play Services, so the app needs no camera permission.
- The token is stored encrypted on the phone and is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.
- A searchable, reorderable entity picker and camera settings.
- A background relay service answers the watch even when the phone app is closed.

## Requirements

| | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Phone | Android 8.0 (API 26) with Google Play Services |
| Watch | Wear OS 3 (API 30) or newer |
| Home Assistant | Reachable from the phone (LAN, VPN or public URL) and a Long-Lived Access Token |

## Getting started

1. Install the **phone APK** on your Android phone and the **watch APK** on your Wear OS watch. Both are attached below and are signed with the same key, which the Wearable Data Layer requires to pair them.
2. On the phone, open WristBridge, enter your Home Assistant URL, and paste or scan a Long-Lived Access Token (*Home Assistant → Profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens*).
3. Pick the entities you want on your wrist and, optionally, a camera.
4. On the watch, long-press the watch face or swipe to the end of your tiles, then add **HA Controls** and/or **HA Camera**.

## Privacy

- The watch app has **no network access**. Everything goes through your own phone.
- WristBridge has no analytics, no telemetry and no third-party servers. The phone only ever talks to the Home Assistant URL you entered.
